Slide 03 / 12 • Fixes 01 & 02 Time required: 3 minutes

Customise Your URL & Verify Your Identity.

Before anyone reads a word of your copy, they see your URL in resumes, email signatures and DMs, and they look for trust signals in your top card.

Fix 01 • URL Hygiene

Claim linkedin.com/in/yourname

  • 1 Strip the default random digits: Never leave a URL like linkedin.com/in/rahul-sharma-89241b2a on your CV or slide deck.
  • 2 How to fix in 30 seconds: Go to your profile → click Edit public profile & URL (top right) → click the pencil icon under Edit your custom URL.
  • 3 If your exact name is taken: Use a clean, memorable modifier like /in/kapildoesmarketing, /in/heyyourname or /in/yourname-gtm (no numbers, no underscores).
Fix 02 • Trust & Top-Card Link

Verification Badge, Audio Intro & Custom Link

  • 1 Get the Verification Tick: Verify via work email or government ID on the LinkedIn mobile app. Verified profiles get higher connection acceptance and DM response rates.
  • 2 Record the 10-Second Audio Intro: On mobile, tap the speaker icon next to your name to record your name pronunciation plus a 7-second elevator pitch.
  • 3 Add a Custom Top-Card CTA Link: Under Edit Intro → Website, add your portfolio, booking link or Links page with custom anchor text (e.g., View Playbooks & Work).
Slide 04 / 12 • Fix 03 + AI Prompt #1 Visual Trust Anchor

Create a Studio-Grade Profile Picture.

You do not need an expensive studio shoot. Use a sharp, well-lit selfie and run this exact prompt in your image model to keep 100% of your natural facial features while upgrading lighting, posture and attire.

What Works on LinkedIn

4 Rules of a High-Trust Headshot

  • 1 60% Frame Fill: Crop from chest/shoulders up so your face is immediately recognizable even inside a tiny 40px comment avatar.
  • 2 Preserve Your Real Face: Never use uncanny AI avatars that alter your face shape or skin texture. Keep your real expression and hair intact.
  • 3 Soft Studio Lighting & Clean Backdrop: A neutral dark or warm minimal background creates instant contrast in both dark and light feeds.
  • 4 Approachable Confidence: Slight natural smile, direct eye contact, and a tailored solid-color blazer or smart-casual layer.
AI Prompt 01 • Studio Headshot 
Use my uploaded selfie as a reference. Keep my face, facial features, expression, skin texture, and hairstyle exactly the same. Do not change my look.

Change only the outfit to a highly professional business outfit (tailored blazer, solid neutral color).

Improve lighting to soft, studio-style professional lighting.

Adjust pose to a professional head-and-shoulders LinkedIn portrait with confident, natural posture.

Use a clean, minimal, premium background.

Make it look like a real professional photoshoot, ultra-realistic and LinkedIn appropriate.

What to Showcase in Your LinkedIn Banner.

Your banner is the largest visual real estate on your profile. Instead of leaving it grey or using a random skyline photo, combine 2 to 3 of these 6 high-signal elements.

01 • Authority Proof

Speaking on Stage

A photo of you speaking on stage, at a conference, hosting a workshop, or on a panel builds instant real-world credibility.

02 • Hard Metrics

Numbers That Matter

Highlight 2-3 concrete proof points: revenue unlocked, leads generated, growth %, hires made, projects shipped, or audience reach.

03 • Clear Direction

Your Current Intent

Tell visitors what you are open to right now: target roles, GTM consulting, AI automation builds, speaking, or founder collaborations.

04 • Positioning

Your Differentiator

Name your signature method, framework, or operating philosophy so visitors understand how you think in 3 seconds.

05 • Next Step

A Soft Call-to-Action

Include a low-friction invitation in the top-right or bottom-right corner (e.g., "DM to collaborate" or "Let's Talk").

06 • Memorability

Personal Brand Cue

A crisp 5-8 word tagline, belief, or signature line (keep the bottom-left 30% clear so your circular avatar never covers text on mobile).

Slide 06 / 12 • Fix 05 + AI Prompt #2 Travels With Every Comment You Write

Write a 15-20 Word Converting Headline.

Your headline appears under your name every time you post, comment or send a connection request. Ditch vague buzzwords and use this 2-part structure.

The Headline Formula

8-Word Hook + 3 Industry Keywords

  • 1 First 8 Words (The Hook): Make the opening conversational, curiosity-driven, or proof-based (a number, milestone, or "I help [audience] achieve [outcome]") because mobile truncates after ~45 characters.
  • 2 Add 3 Exact Industry Keywords: Frame them to clearly show what you do, for whom, and the result you deliver.
  • 3 Include Metrics or Outcomes: If you have experience, anchor it with a real number (e.g., "100+ automations shipped", "6+ yrs across Big Tech & MNCs").
  • 4 Zero Buzzwords: Ban empty fillers like "Passionate", "Thought Leader", "Guru", or "Results-oriented".
AI Prompt 02 • Headline Architect 
Act as a LinkedIn branding expert. Write one LinkedIn headline (15-20 words) using this format:

- First 8 words: conversational, curiosity-driven, or proof-based (number, milestone, or "I help...")
- Add exactly 3 industry keywords. Frame them to clearly show what I do, for whom, and the result I deliver. Include metrics or outcomes if experienced. Keep it clear, human, and skimmable. Avoid buzzwords.

Note: Please ask 5 highly relevant questions which will help you write a perfect converting LinkedIn profile headline for me and come up with at least 3 different options. Do not write the headlines until I answer your 5 questions.
Slide 07 / 12 • Fix 07 + AI Prompt #3 Your 20-Second Pitch

Write a Converting "About Me" Story.

A great About section starts with your audience's problem, not your childhood biography. Use this fill-in-the-blank blueprint or run the 10-question AI copywriter prompt.

The Fill-in-the-Blank Blueprint

Problem → Role → Method → Proof → CTA

[Target audience] often face [core problem], which results in [impact].

I work as [your role], helping [who you help] achieve [key outcome] through [method/approach].

My background includes [experience/industries], shaped by [key skills or insights]. I believe in [core principles] and focus on [how you create value].

Currently open to [jobs/consulting/collaborations]. Best way to connect: [DM/email/link].

Slide 09 / 12 • Fix 09 + AI Prompt #4 LinkedIn Recruiter Search Engine

Add 50+ Relevant Skills & Pin Your Top 5.

Recruiters and founders use boolean skill filters in LinkedIn Recruiter. If a skill is not listed on your profile, you are invisible in those searches.

How LinkedIn Skill SEO Works

4 Buckets + Top 5 Pinning

  • 1 Pin Your Top 5 Skills: LinkedIn lets you reorder and pin 5 flagship skills at the top of your Skills section (and inside your About card). Pick the 5 skills you want to be hired for tomorrow.
  • 2 Tie Skills to Specific Roles: Link each skill to the job, internship, or certification where you used it. LinkedIn weights role-linked skills higher in search.
  • 3 Cover All 4 Skill Categories: Core functional skills, Tools & platforms, Industry-specific terms, and Transferable leadership skills.
  • 4 Cut Outdated Fillers: Replace generic tags like "Microsoft Word" with high-intent skills recruiters actually filter for.
AI Prompt 04 • 50-100 SEO Skills Generator 
Act as a LinkedIn profile optimization expert. Your task is to suggest 50-100 relevant LinkedIn skills that I should add to my skills section. The skills must match my current experience + target job roles.

Requirements:
- Be relevant to my industry.
- Include a mix of core functional skills, tools/platform skills (if applicable), industry-specific skills, and transferable skills.
- Be optimized for recruiter search and LinkedIn SEO.
- Avoid generic or outdated skills.

Before generating the list, ask me 3-5 relevant professional questions to understand my experience, industry, and target roles.

After I answer, provide a clean categorized list (50-100 skills) plus recommend which Top 5 skills I should pin first. Do not generate the skills until I respond.
Slide 10 / 12 • Fixes 10, 11, 12 & 13 + AI Prompt #5 Credibility Stack

Experience, Education, Certifications & Volunteering.

Do not just list job titles or copy-paste HR responsibilities. Show the problems you owned, the systems you shipped, and the measurable outcomes you created.

Fixes 10-13 • Proof Architecture

How to Structure Each Section

  • 10 Experience (Jobs, Internships, Ventures): Write 3-4 impact bullets per role using Action + System + Metric, and attach media links or project decks underneath.
  • 11 Education: Include societies, case competitions, leadership roles, or capstone projects rather than just the degree name.
  • 12 Certifications: Add high-signal industry credentials with verified credential URLs so recruiters can validate technical depth.
  • 13 Volunteer Commitments: Mentoring, community organizing, or pro-bono work signals initiative and character beyond your 9-to-5.
AI Prompt 05 • Experience Bullet Rewriter 
Act as an executive career coach and LinkedIn copywriter. I want to rewrite my LinkedIn Experience section so it reads like a track record of outcomes rather than a list of job duties.

Ask me 4 quick questions about a role (my title, what I actually built or owned, the tools/skills used, and any rough numbers, time saved, or business results).

Once I reply, write:
1. A 1-sentence scope summary of the role.
2. 3-4 punchy bullet points using the "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]" framework.
3. Top 5 skills to tag onto this specific role.
Slide 11 / 12 • Fixes 14 & 15 + Outreach Template 45 Minutes Per Week

The Weekly Compounding Habit & Recommendations.

An optimized profile is your conversion engine, but activity is the traffic source. Follow this simple weekly cadence so the right people discover your profile every week.

Fixes 14 & 15 • Weekly Rhythm

1x Post + 3x Comments + Social Proof

  • 1 Post or Repost With Insight 1x a Week: Share one real takeaway, teardown, project learning, or industry perspective every week. Consistency beats virality.
  • 2 Comment 3x a Week: Leave thoughtful 2-3 sentence comments on posts by founders, hiring managers, and peers in your space. Remember: your new headline travels with every comment!
  • 3 Ask for Recommendations Proactively: Written recommendations at the bottom of your profile are third-party proof that you deliver results. Aim for at least 3 strong recommendations.
Bonus Template • Recommendation Request DM 
Hey [Name], hope you're doing well!

I really enjoyed working together on [Project / Role / Initiative] - especially when we tackled [specific win or milestone].

I'm currently updating my LinkedIn profile and would be super grateful if you'd be open to writing a short 2-3 sentence LinkedIn recommendation highlighting our work on [specific skill or project outcome].

No pressure at all, and if it helps save time, I'm happy to share a quick bullet reminder of what we shipped together. And I'd love to write one for your profile in return as well!

Overview

What the summit was, and why it mattered

How India plans a holiday has changed more in two years than in the previous ten. On 30 October 2026, Google India brought the leaders steering that change into one room at Google Gurgaon: airlines, online travel agencies, hotel groups, mobility brands, creators and agencies.

Think Travel 2026 was built around one theme: how AI is reshaping the traveller's journey from the first spark of inspiration to the final booking. The day opened with the launch of the Think Travel Report, Google India's research with Kantar, then moved through a Leader Circle with MakeMyTrip, IndiGo and Air India, a first look at AI Mode and agentic search, and a Creator's Corner on why trips now start on YouTube Shorts. With the Travel sector team, I owned the communications and visual design that carried the summit from the first invite to the closing slide.

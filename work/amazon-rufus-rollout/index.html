Overview

What the rollout was, and why it mattered

In February 2024 Amazon introduced Rufus, a generative AI shopping assistant inside the Amazon app. I led its rollout to sellers in the United States childcare vertical: a zero-to-one programme where the concept had to be pitched, sellers onboarded, and their listings adjusted so the assistant could answer shoppers well.

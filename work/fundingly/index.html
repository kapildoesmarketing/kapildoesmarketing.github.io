Overview

What it is, and why I built it

Indian startup funding data sits behind institutional terminals that cost thousands of dollars a year, or scattered across press releases that repeat and contradict each other. The founders, analysts and students who need it most cannot pay for it.

Fundingly.in is a free, searchable record of Indian startup funding rounds. Every week it collects news, investor updates and public filings, removes duplicates and rumours, and publishes verified rounds with company profiles and trend reports. I built it alone: the research process, the data model, the website and the short-form video engine that turns each week into social posts.

What I did

Three things I owned end to end

Replaced a paywall with a spreadsheet

Designed a 21-quarter Google Sheets database that one person can update in under three minutes a week, so the data stays free.

Made the data trustworthy

Built a multi-source check that removed 290+ duplicate press releases and rumours before any round went live.

Made it fast and free

Pre-built the search index so filtering by stage, sector, city or investor happens in the browser in under 50 milliseconds.
